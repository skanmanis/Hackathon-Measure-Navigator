This file contains synthetic hackathon content and must be replaced by the office-approved MY2026 specification before testing with organizational artifacts.

## Multiple blood pressure readings on the same date

When multiple blood pressure readings occur on the same date, use the lowest systolic value and the lowest diastolic value from that date to determine the representative result. The selected systolic and diastolic values do not need to come from the same reading. For this synthetic demonstration, controlled blood pressure requires systolic below 140 and diastolic below 90.

## Acute inpatient and emergency department settings

A blood pressure reading taken during an acute inpatient stay or emergency department visit is not accepted as numerator evidence in this synthetic demonstration. Confirm the care setting before applying the reading-selection rule.
